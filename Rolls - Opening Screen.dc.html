Rolls OPENING SCREEN

The front door — your Shelf: a logbook of every roll, each with its own film-cartridge icon, and “load a new roll” as the primary action. Swipe left into the Cosmos: a single continuous zoom from your frames on a timeline, out to a map of where you shot, all the way to the globe adrift among the stars. Mobile and desktop, both on the chosen ledger direction. All live — drag the swipe track, pinch to travel days → map → globe.

Mobile — Your Shelf
The Shelf · logbook index Dense darkroom ledger — each roll carries its own film-cartridge icon. “Load a new roll” pinned to the bottom.
Frame logging Live in-app: tap a roll on the Shelf to open it; “‹ Your Shelf” goes back. Theme changes apply here too.
Mobile — Cosmos · Days → Places → Earth
Days · calendar Photo-tile months floating in space. Scroll through months; pinch out to fly to the map.
Places · real map A real, draggable map (live tiles) with photo stacks where frames were shot. Tap a stack to expand → jump to that frame; zoom out far enough and it becomes the Earth.
Earth · 3D globe A real 3D Earth — drag to spin it; tap a photo stack (or pinch in) to fly back into the map.
Desktop

Cosmos · globe Real 3D Earth on the star field; pinch-zoom travels calendar → map → globe.
